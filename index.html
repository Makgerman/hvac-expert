SERVICES

Engineering services

We work with operating facilities as well as systems being prepared for handover. The scope of each visit is agreed after reviewing the equipment and the task.

01

Technical inspections

Engineering surveys of villas and commercial properties, pre-purchase inspections, defect identification, equipment condition assessments and written recommendations.

02

VRV / VRF diagnostics and repair

Troubleshooting Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric and other systems: fault codes, refrigerant circuits, electrical connections, sensors, controls and operating parameters.

03

Ventilation and air treatment

Inspection and servicing of supply and exhaust ventilation, air handling units, fans, filters, dampers and control components. Checking airflow and system operation.

04

Preventive maintenance

Seasonal and scheduled servicing of air-conditioning equipment, heat pumps, fan coils and chillers, including operating checks, cleaning and maintenance planning.

05

Commissioning and controls

Equipment start-up, testing, adjustment and troubleshooting of HVAC control circuits. Support during handover and correction of installation defects.

06

Water systems and pool equipment

Troubleshooting and repair of heating and water-supply equipment, pumps, valves and domestic hot-water systems. Pool circulation, filtration and technical maintenance.
